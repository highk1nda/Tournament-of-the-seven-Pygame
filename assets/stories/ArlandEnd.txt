He had done it, this was his final fight. He wasn’t shocked, he had been fighting against knights like the Knights Templar all his life.
He looked to the gods, confident and arrogant.
“You know what I want,” he declared.
The gods didn’t know whether to be impressed by it or offended, regardless they granted his wish. On the morrow, he was Arland Bowsbury in 
charge of a major castle in the realm, full of crops and surrounded by potential wives. He didn’t need to kill anymore, it was another 
instance of a noble house whose origins were drenched in blood. He stood on his balcony, overlooking the fields where peasants bent beneath 
the sun and worked for land and crops that weren’t truly theirs. Arland grinned, at last he had seized what he never could with gold or arrows, 
power.
