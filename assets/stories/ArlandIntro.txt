Arland is a commoner but a skilled marksman who craved the taste of the finer things. He was once a soldier, however he found out fighting in an 
army paid less than being a killer for hire... no one saw him, no one could track him down, and no one escaped him. A person went to him, 
paid the required gold, said a name, and the person who that name belonged to never saw the light of day again. However gold can only buy so much, 
it didn't buy him what truly separated the commoners from the noble, the right last name. He enters the tournament to ask for lordship of fertile 
lands.
"Never much liked killing, but I do like gold"
