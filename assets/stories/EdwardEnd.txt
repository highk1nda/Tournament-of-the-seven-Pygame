It was over, Ser Edward knelt in the presence of the gods. 
"Your wish, champion," the gods declared.
"A lawful divorce," he said, without a moment's hesitation. “Let her keep the house." There was silence as the gods exchanged glances in 
confusion. In ten thousand years of granting wishes, nobody had ever asked for paperwork. Certainly not after facing a Knights Templar.
It was done. That evening, Ser Edward sat across from Margaret at the dinner table for the last time. They ate in silence, no arguments, 
no awkward attempts at a conversation, just comfortable silence. 
"You were a terrible husband," she said.
"You were a terrible wife," he replied.
Soon the old warrior was back on the battlefield. He was, by all accounts, annoyingly cheerful about it.
