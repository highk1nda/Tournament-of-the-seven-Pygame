Ser Edward is a seasoned warrior, he has faced werewolves, dragons, orcs, even the undead. He was revered across the 
kingdom of Raphalia as a dashing knight in his younger years and a seasoned effective general in his glory days. Nothing 
during his 40 years of experience has managed to break his spirit, until he got married that is. Ser Edward thought it time 
to hang up his sword and start a family. A simple life he thought, much deserved after so much chaos. Unfortunately not all 
things are destined to work out and his wife, Margaret, turned out to be infertile. She wasn't a villain. She wasn't cruel. 
She was simply a woman who wanted a husband present at dinner, but it seemed Edward had idealized this future with a wife 
and son and when that future was shattered, kept reaching for a sword that wasn't on his hip anymore. The silence of a 
warm home was somehow louder than any battlefield he'd known. He knocked things over. He forgot anniversaries. He even 
sharpened his butter knife out of habit. But an oath is an oath. Ser Edward had never broken one in his life. So as
one does, he immediately took the opportunity when whispers reached him of a divine tournament, a competition where the 
gods themselves would grant a single wish to the victor. Ser Edward was going to risk his life for a lawful divorce.
He dusted off his armor, sharpened his sword, and set out to the tournament. This knight was not done fighting. Not just yet.
"Even if I die in the tournament, I'm sure it's a better fate than enduring any more of this."
