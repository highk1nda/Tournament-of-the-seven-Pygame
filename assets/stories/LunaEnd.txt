She shouldn’t have won by any means. She shouldn't have been able to best a Knights Templar, but something burned inside her that day, fierce 
and determined. She won, and looked down at her staff… broken. The last piece of her mentor broke in her hands.
“Your wish, champion” the gods asked.
Luna thought about it. For the first time she finally understood as she stood breathless, she had never felt so alive. The citadel taught 
her magic as a science, a discipline, theorems, theses, graded examinations. Somewhere along the way she had stopped feeling what she felt 
when William the wise would show her new spells. The wonder of magic, the joy was being stripped from her without her realizing it. Perhaps 
laziness wasn’t a hurdle, it was a part of her subconscious telling her something.
“I wish for my staff to be repaired” she declared.
The staff became one again, the wood warmed in her hand like usual. She did not go back to the citadel, she hit the road, from village to 
village, from chore to chore helping people the way William led his life before her.
