She won, in the way Rem always wins, not viciously, but with an inevitability that left her annoyed and her opponent politely reconsidering their 
choices. She stood before the gods and made her wish. “The maze and the forest around it were sacred ground,” declared the gods. From that 
point on all were prohibited to enter, all but the gentle giant and so Rem walked home. The rabbits were waiting, the birds sang to her as if 
they were saying welcome back. Her bed of flowers grew, and the trees had grown some apples and pears. Rem laid down and finally slept, 
uninterrupted. Somewhere, a young adventurer looked towards the forest, begrudgingly frowned, and decided that slaying monsters was not his 
calling, instead he took up pottery… he was quite good at it.
