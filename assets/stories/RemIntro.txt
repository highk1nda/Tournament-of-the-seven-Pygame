A peaceful sleepy loveable giant who is remarkably shy and loves nature. She was hunted all her life for being the monster when humanity was the 
primary aggressor. She eventually got adopted by a talented builder who named her Rem and before dying made her a home no one would disturb her 
in, a maze in the forest, where she could eat grass and sleep in a bed of flowers. However, humanity still found a way to torment her.
Rumors began spreading in the nearby villages that a monster was living in the forest, parents would tell their kids horror stories, any time 
someone got mauled by a wolf and was embarrassed they said the minotaur was to blame. This emboldened young adventurers to be the "hero that 
slayed the monster" and brought the villages its head so that their children would sleep peacefully. Any time one of those stuck up brats enters 
her maze with their sword and big words like "I'm gonna slay you monster" rem is forced to make quick work of them. Rem did not choose to be 
frightening, she chose to be peaceful and gentle. Rem hates killing and grows sadder each time she is forced to defend herself, she just wants 
to spend her immortality in her maze, enjoying the nature around her. When word reached her of a tournament where the gods give the victor a 
wish, she knew she had to take it.  She takes part in this tournament to ask the gods to prohibit humanity from entering her maze.
