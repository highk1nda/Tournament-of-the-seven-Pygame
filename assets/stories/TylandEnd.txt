Tyland won, he had bested a Knights Templar, it seemed hard to believe that he was once beaten by the butcher's boy. Thinking about it, the 
witch technically kept her promise, who would have thought he would be begging the gods to make him his weak old self again. He didn’t need to 
say a word, not that he could. The gods fulfilled the wish quietly. One moment there was a monster and the next a young man laying naked in the 
dirt. He looked at his hands for a long time. Regular hands. Embarrassingly small, compared to what he was used to. He laughed. Then, 
mortifyingly cried at his weak body. Then he laughed again. Tyland went back to his family for an extremely awkward reunion dinner. He did not 
reclaim his old arrogance. That, it seemed, the witch had kept.
