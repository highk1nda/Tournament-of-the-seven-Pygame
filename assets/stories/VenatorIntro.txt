Only 7 men have had the honor to call themselves templars, Knights Templar renounce their name and their sins in order to fight threats that 
plague the human realm, each knight has the blessing of the gods, blessed with remarkable strength these skilled warriors fight anyone who 
challenges the kingdom of heaven. These knights don't age, they can only die by being stricken down, so far none have. Cities on the brink of 
fall rejoice on their arrival. They all acquired different names after becoming templar, this one's name is Venator. 
 "In the name of Lympos and its divine kingdom, FALL!"
